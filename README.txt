TIKTOK TV WEBSITE — PUBLISHING NOTES

Files:
- index.html   = Web/Desktop URL (homepage)
- terms.html   = Terms of Service URL
- privacy.html = Privacy Policy URL

Before publishing:
1. Open terms.html and replace:
   [YOUR LEGAL NAME OR ORGANIZATION]
   [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL ADDRESS]
2. Open privacy.html and replace:
   [YOUR LEGAL NAME OR ORGANIZATION]
   [YOUR PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]
3. Carefully rewrite the privacy sections so they match the actual app,
   including all SDKs, analytics, hosting, TikTok scopes, data retention,
   sharing, and deletion practices.
4. Do not submit placeholder text to TikTok. These are starter templates,
   not legal advice or a claim that your app has particular data practices.

Free GitHub Pages publishing:
1. Sign in at https://github.com/ or create a free account.
2. Create a new PUBLIC repository named: tiktok-tv
3. Upload index.html, terms.html, and privacy.html to the repository root.
4. Open repository Settings > Pages.
5. Under Build and deployment, choose Deploy from a branch.
6. Choose branch main and folder /(root), then Save.
7. Wait for GitHub Pages to publish.

Your URLs will be:
https://YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME.github.io/tiktok-tv/
https://YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME.github.io/tiktok-tv/terms.html
https://YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME.github.io/tiktok-tv/privacy.html

The first URL is the Web/Desktop URL. The second is the Terms of Service URL.
The third is the Privacy Policy URL.
